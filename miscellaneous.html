THE COVENANT

The Covenant Empire (simply known as the Covenant), is a multi-species empire that was united by the worship of the ancient Forerunners and the idea of their "Great Journey" to activate the Halo rings. They believed that the Halo rings were the key to the Prophets of the Covenant becoming godlike figures. However, the Flood brought the Covenant to its downfall, and because of the civil war between the Elites and the Brutes, two species in the Covenant, went to war. The Prophet of Truth, The Prophet of Mercy, and The Prophet of Regret led the Covenant during the events of Halo CE-3. The Covenant consists of Brutes, Elites, Grunts, Jackals, Drones, and Hunters.

FUN FACT!

The Human-Covenant War lasted for 27-28 years, beginning with the Battle of Harvest on February 11th, 2525.
